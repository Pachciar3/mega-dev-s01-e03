#4471 says one issue, one fix queued. Read report by report, the reports merged into it show three different problems. The queued fix is for one of them. Each problem now has its own ticket.

When the parser fix ships, two of these problems stay. Do not close their tickets with #4471.

What the reports show

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Day-first tenants write 7/4 for 7 April; the parser reads 4 July. Day and month swap. This is the bug the #4471 page describes, and the queued fix is for it. It reaches every channel.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People do what the hint says, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks exactly like parser: day and month swap. It only happens on the form of booking-v3 tenants, and the parser fix does not touch it. The hint and the form must agree.

shift: the date moves by one day

These reports describe a date one day off, not a swap: “Tuesday 12 May” in the invite, Monday 11 May in the calendar; one meeting on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite that arrives before the booking was made. A month/day swap cannot produce that. It looks like a time zone conversion, and neither fix above touches it. The desk had already listed these reports (desk-notes.json) but left them under parser by tenant.

Why they ended up in one ticket

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. Every problem here produces the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Dates mixed up”), so subject matching cannot tell them apart.
  • Intake’s tag comes from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. The “intake agreed” column shows how often it matched. Use the tenant, its form and the channel (causes.ts), and read the body.
  • The ticket export pages overlap. Count reports by id, not by row.

For the desk

  • Merge into a ticket by cause, not by subject. A replacement for decision 0001 is needed.
  • When a body describes a different move (one day, not a swap), file it apart even if the tenant fits a known cause.
